P/04 · Where this site began2025 · Previous portfolio

Portfolio v1 — “Universe”

My first portfolio was a NASA-style solar system: every planet was a doorway to a page, with a built-in AI chatbot. Building it taught me React Three Fiber, real-time 3D and how much a site can say before anyone reads a word.

This site is what I learned next — one particle system instead of 30 MB of textures, a story you scroll instead of a map you hunt through, and content that works on every screen. I keep v1 live as a milestone of how far it’s come.
